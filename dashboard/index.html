How implied value is computed

implied = round( size(TVL) × tilt × growth × maturity × reach )

Size curve
Fitted from this snapshot’s live tokens (see below)
Category tilt
Each category’s median token price, against the market
Growth
7d change clamped to −60% … +60%; down weeks weigh 0.50, up weeks 0.40
Maturity & reach
0.6 + 0.4 × (1 − e−age/365); 1 + 0.02 per extra chain, max 1.10

One function scores every row; no protocol name is special-cased. Membership follows the watchlist’s own rule as far as the snapshot’s ten fields allow: pre-token, not an exchange, no version marker in the name. The snapshot carries no parent-protocol field, so a curator of an existing protocol cannot be told apart from a standalone one.

The fit, and how it was checked

The size curve is a log-log fit of market cap on TVL across the live-token protocols in this snapshot, and each category’s tilt is its median market-cap-to-TVL ratio (winsorized to 0.01–3.00, pulled toward the market median when the category has fewer than 4 live tokens).
